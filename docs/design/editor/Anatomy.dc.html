Wires and snapping

Shape and stroke carry the signal type, so colour is never the only cue.

Audio wire Solid amber, circle to circle. Signal flows left to right.
Modulation wire · Proposed Dashed blue, always lands on a diamond.
Dragging Every port that could take the wire gets a ring.
Snapped Inside 24 px the end jumps to the port and names it.
Rejected A wire that would form a cycle is refused before release.
Replace Dropping on a used input swaps out the old wire.
